When should I go to a hospital instead in Legian?

Go to the nearest emergency room for fainting, blood in stool or vomit, severe or worsening abdominal pain, a very high fever, confusion, or if you cannot keep any fluids down. Babies, elderly people and pregnant women should be assessed urgently.

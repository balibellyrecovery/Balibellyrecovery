Bali Belly Recovery – Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about home visits, treatment packages and booking.

How many Bali Belly treatment packages are available?

There are 16 package options in two groups: B-Complex and Multivitamin. Options vary by additional components such as additional Electrolyte Solution (total 1,000 ml), fever reducer (antipyretic) and oral medication. The doctor will assess the patient and confirm clinically appropriate care.

Are the prices shown promotional prices?

Yes. All prices displayed on this page include a 15% discount and cover the services and components listed in your selected package. There are no extra charges for the listed inclusions.

What is included in the service?

The service information includes a nurse and doctor visit, doctor consultation, medical report document and 24/7 on-call medical support. Treatment and medication are subject to individual doctor assessment and clinical suitability.

Do all packages include oral medication?

No. Oral medication is included only in selected packages and may include Anti-Diarrhea Medication, Anti-Nausea/Vomiting Medication, and Stomach Cramp Medication. Antibiotics are included only if medically indicated and prescribed by the doctor.

Can you visit my villa, hotel or holiday rental?

You can request a doctor and nurse visit to a villa, hotel or holiday rental in the listed service areas. The team must confirm the exact location and availability before accepting the visit.

What if my symptoms are severe or getting worse?

Do not wait for a home visit if you have fainting, confusion, severe or worsening abdominal pain, blood in stool or vomit, or signs of serious dehydration. Seek urgent medical care at the nearest emergency department.

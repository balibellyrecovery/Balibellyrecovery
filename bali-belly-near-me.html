Frequently asked questions

Is there a Bali Belly doctor near me?

Yes. Bali Belly Recovery sends a doctor and nurse to your villa or hotel, 24 hours a day, in 15 areas of Bali from Canggu and Seminyak to Uluwatu, Ubud and Tabanan. Send your WhatsApp location pin and we confirm your estimated arrival time.

Is there a Bali Belly clinic near me I can walk into?

Bali Belly Recovery is a home-visit service. The doctor and nurse come to your villa or hotel, so you do not have to travel while unwell. This is often faster and more comfortable than finding a clinic near you.

How do I find Bali Belly treatment near me fast?

Message us on WhatsApp at +62 822 2161 4966 with your location pin, villa or hotel name, and your symptoms. We confirm the booking and an estimated arrival time. You can also use the form on our appointment page.

Is Bali Belly treatment near me available at night?

Yes. Our doctor and nurse home visits are available 24 hours a day across the areas we cover.

Can I get an IV drip for Bali Belly near me?

Yes, when the doctor recommends it after assessing you. The Bali Belly Package includes an electrolyte solution and supportive medication, and IV therapy is given as the doctor judges appropriate.

Which areas does Bali Belly near me cover?

We cover 15 areas: Canggu, Kerobokan & Umalas, Dalung, Seminyak, Legian, Kuta, Munggu & Cemagi, Kedungu & Tanah Lot, Tabanan, Uluwatu & Pecatu, Jimbaran, Nusa Dua & Benoa, Sanur, Denpasar, Ubud.

What is Bali Belly?

Bali Belly is a common name for traveller’s diarrhoea and stomach upset in Bali, often with nausea, vomiting, cramps and dehydration. A doctor assesses what you need.

How much is Bali Belly treatment near me?

The three Bali Belly packages are priced at Rp 2.025.000 for Essential, Rp 2.275.000 for Bali Belly + Tablet Medication, and Rp 2.525.000 for Complete. Current package prices and any active promotion are displayed in the package list and confirmed when booking. Confirm the offer when booking.

When is it an emergency?

Go to the nearest emergency room for fainting, blood in stool or vomit, severe or worsening abdominal pain, a very high fever, confusion, or if you cannot keep any fluids down.

This is not an emergency service. If you faint, are confused, have severe or worsening abdominal pain, blood in stool or vomit, a very high fever, or cannot keep any fluids down, go to the nearest emergency department or call 112 instead of waiting for a home visit.

1. The service

We arrange doctor and nurse home visits to villas, hotels and holiday rentals in our listed Bali service areas, mainly for non-emergency conditions such as Bali Belly. Visits are available 24 hours a day, every day, subject to the points below.

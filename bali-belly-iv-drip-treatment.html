BALI BELLY IV DRIP

Bali Belly IV drip, at your villa or hotel

A doctor and nurse visit you in Bali. An IV drip is given only when the doctor judges it appropriate after assessing you. Requests are accepted 24/7.

WHAT AN IV DRIP DOES

Gives fluids and electrolytes directly into the bloodstream, which can correct dehydration faster than drinking when you cannot keep fluids down. It does not cure the infection itself.

WHEN A DOCTOR MAY RECOMMEND ONE
  • Dehydration is moderate or worse
  • Oral rehydration is not working
  • You are vomiting too much to keep fluids down
WHAT A VISIT INCLUDES
  • Doctor consultation and assessment
  • IV drip, when appropriate
  • Symptom relief medication matched to your symptoms
  • Medical report for travel insurance claim support

Not everyone needs an IV drip

Bali Belly causes fluid and salt loss through diarrhoea and vomiting. In mild cases, drinking small, frequent amounts of water or oral rehydration solution is often enough. Whether an IV is appropriate is a medical decision made after an assessment. Read more about symptoms and warning signs and whether you actually need an IV drip.

How an IV drip home visit works

  • You message us on WhatsApp with your location and symptoms.
  • We confirm your booking and the team confirms the estimated arrival time for your exact address.
  • The doctor assesses you and decides what care is appropriate: oral fluids and advice, medication, or an IV drip.
  • You receive a medical report. Insurers have different claim rules, so check with yours directly.

Signs you should be assessed urgently

We are not an emergency service. Go to the nearest emergency room for fainting, confusion, blood in stool or vomit, severe or worsening abdominal pain, very high fever, or inability to keep any fluids down.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an IV drip for Bali Belly?

Not everyone does. In mild cases, small frequent sips of water or oral rehydration solution are often enough. A doctor may consider an IV drip when dehydration is moderate or worse, or when you cannot keep fluids down. The decision is made after an assessment.

Can I get an IV drip at my villa or hotel in Bali?

Yes, in our 15 covered areas. A doctor and nurse visit your villa, hotel or holiday rental. If the doctor judges an IV drip appropriate after assessing you, it is given where you are staying.

Does an IV drip cure Bali Belly?

No. An IV drip gives fluids and electrolytes directly into the bloodstream, which can correct dehydration faster than drinking when you cannot keep fluids down. It does not cure the infection itself. Medication such as anti-nausea treatment may be given alongside it if the doctor judges it appropriate.

Is an IV drip always given?

No. The doctor decides what care is appropriate after assessing you. That may be oral fluids and advice, medication, or an IV drip, and some patients are advised on oral fluids and other care instead.

Is the IV drip service available 24/7?

Requests are accepted 24/7. Each visit depends on address confirmation, clinical assessment and team availability, and we confirm the estimated arrival time for your exact address before the team comes.

When should I go to the emergency room instead?

Go to the nearest emergency room for fainting, confusion, blood in stool or vomit, severe or worsening abdominal pain, very high fever, or if you cannot keep any fluids down. A home visit is not a substitute for emergency care.

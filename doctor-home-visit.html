BALI BELLY DOCTOR HOME VISIT

A doctor and nurse at your villa or hotel

Available 24/7. Our team will confirm the estimated arrival time after checking your exact address and medical team availability.

WHAT TO EXPECT
  1. Message us on WhatsApp with your location and symptoms.
  2. We confirm your booking.
  3. The team confirms the estimated arrival time with you before the visit.
  4. You are assessed and treated, and receive a medical report.
HAVE READY
  • Your location or villa address
  • Symptoms and when they started
  • Current medication and allergies
  • Travel insurance details, if any
WHERE WE VISIT

Villas and hotels in all 15 covered areas, from Canggu and Seminyak to Uluwatu, Ubud and Tabanan. See all covered areas.

Free delivery with the package. English-speaking doctors.

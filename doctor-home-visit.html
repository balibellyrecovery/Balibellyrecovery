Who a doctor home visit suits

A home visit is designed for adults who are unwell with Bali Belly or a similar stomach upset, are stable, and would rather be assessed where they are staying than travel to a clinic. The doctor decides what care is appropriate after assessing you. Read more about Bali Belly symptoms and warning signs.

How a visit works

  • You message us on WhatsApp with your location and symptoms.
  • We confirm your booking.
  • The team confirms the estimated arrival time with you after checking your exact address and team availability.
  • The doctor assesses you and treats you according to that assessment. See what treatment can include.
  • You receive a medical report. Insurers have different claim rules, so check with yours directly.

What a home visit is not

We are not a walk-in clinic and not an emergency service. If you have any of the urgent warning signs, such as fainting, confusion, blood in stool or vomit, or severe abdominal pain, go to the nearest emergency room instead of waiting for a visit.

Frequently asked questions

Is the doctor home visit available 24/7?

Appointment requests are accepted 24/7. Each visit depends on address confirmation, clinical assessment and team availability, and we confirm the estimated arrival time for your exact address before the team comes.

How do I book a doctor home visit in Bali?

Message us on WhatsApp at +62 896 7009 8140 with your location pin, your symptoms and when they started. We confirm your booking, then the team confirms the estimated arrival time with you before the visit.

Where do you visit?

We visit villas, hotels and holiday rentals in 15 covered areas, from Canggu and Seminyak to Uluwatu, Ubud and Tabanan. Send a WhatsApp location pin to confirm that your address is covered.

Do the doctors speak English?

Yes. Our doctors speak English.

Is this a clinic I can walk into?

No. Bali Belly Recovery is an on-call home-visit service. The doctor and nurse come to your villa or hotel after you send your location.

What should I have ready before the team arrives?

Your location or villa address, your symptoms and when they started, your current medication and allergies, and your travel insurance details if you have any.

What if my symptoms are severe?

A home visit is not a substitute for emergency services. For fainting, confusion, blood in stool or vomit, severe or worsening abdominal pain, very high fever, or if you cannot keep any fluids down, go to the nearest emergency room.
